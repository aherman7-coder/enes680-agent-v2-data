# Annual vs Monthly Income Toggle
**T-08** | Harbor Market | Income/Housing | Oct 20 to Nov 17, 2025 | Idea source: Partner request

## What we tested
A toggle letting applicants enter income as monthly or annual. Control only accepts annual.

## Why
Harbor Market's support team reported applicants entering monthly pay in the annual field, which triggers declines or manual review.

## Results
- Results look directionally positive. Fewer income values under $5,000 were entered in treatment.
- Final lift numbers pending; analysis was not completed before the analyst moved to the Rewards team.
- Sample size: not recorded in this deck.

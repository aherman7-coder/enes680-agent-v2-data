# Test Readout: Review Page Summary Card
**T-03** | Branded card | Review & submit | May 12 to Jun 9, 2025 | Idea source: Branded

## What we tested
Replaced the full-length review page (every answer listed, ~2.5 screens on mobile) with a single editable summary card grouped by section.

## Hypothesis
A shorter, scannable review step will increase submits.

## Design
- 50/50 split, all branded card traffic
- Sample: 72,400 applicants
- Primary metric: submit rate

## Results
| Metric | Control | Treatment | Lift | Significant? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Review step completion | 88.4% | 90.9% | +2.8% relative | Yes (p < 0.01) |
| Submit rate | 55.1% | 56.5% | +2.6% relative | Yes (p < 0.01) |
| Edits made on review | 14% | 11% | n/a | n/a |

## Learnings
- Biggest gain on mobile; desktop lift was +0.9%.
- Rolled out to 100% of branded traffic July 2025.
- Recommend replicating on partner flows.

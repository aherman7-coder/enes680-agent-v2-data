# Readout: Income Helper Tooltip
**T-02** | Harbor Market | Income step | Apr 14 to May 5, 2025 | Idea source: Internal

## What we tested
An "i" icon next to the income field opened a tooltip listing what counts as income (salary, retirement, spousal income if accessible, etc.).

## Hypothesis
Clarifying what counts as income will reduce abandonment on the income question.

## Design
- 50/50 split, Harbor Market partner flow
- Sample: 9,800 applicants total
- Primary metric: income step completion
- Ran 3 weeks (planned 4; ended early for a partner release freeze)

## Results
| Metric | Control | Treatment | Lift | Significant? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Income step completion | 86.0% | 87.8% | +2.1% relative | Yes (p = 0.04) |
| Submit rate | 49.8% | 50.0% | +0.4% relative | No |

## Notes
- Only 6% of treatment applicants opened the tooltip.
- Analyst flagged the shortened run and small sample; recommended a rerun on higher-traffic flow before rollout.
- Decision: not rolled out. Rerun queued (see T-12).

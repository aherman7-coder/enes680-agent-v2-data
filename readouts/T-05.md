# Readout: Inline SSN Explanation
**T-05** | Harbor Market | Identity verification | Jul 14 to Aug 11, 2025 | Idea source: Competitor

## What we tested
Two lines of copy under the SSN field explaining why it's needed (to verify identity and check credit, soft pull only before submit) and a lock icon with "encrypted and never shared with Harbor Market."

## Design
- 50/50 split, Harbor Market flow
- Sample: 22,600 applicants
- Primary metric: identity verification step completion

## Results
| Metric | Control | Treatment | Lift | Significant? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ID verification step completion | 78.3% | 80.7% | +3.1% relative | Yes (p < 0.01) |
| Submit rate | 47.9% | 48.4% | +1.0% relative | No (p = 0.11) |

## Learnings
- Strongest step-level lift in the 2025 program.
- Submit lift directionally positive but underpowered; recommend rolling out and monitoring.
- Partner (Harbor Market) legal approved the "never shared" language. Other partners would need their own review.

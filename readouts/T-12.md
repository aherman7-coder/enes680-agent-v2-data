# Readout: Income Helper Tooltip (Rerun)
**T-12** | Branded card | Income | May 4 to Jun 1, 2026 | Idea source: Internal (rerun of T-02)

## What we tested
Same income tooltip as T-02, on branded traffic with a full 4-week run.

## Design
- 50/50 split, branded traffic
- Sample: 61,000 applicants
- Primary metric: income step completion

## Results
| Metric | Control | Treatment | Lift | Significant? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Income step completion | 89.3% | 89.1% | -0.2% relative | No |
| Submit rate | 55.8% | 55.9% | +0.2% relative | No |

## Learnings
- No effect at a sample 6x larger than T-02. The T-02 lift was likely noise from a short, small test.
- Tooltip open rate was 4%; applicants don't seek out help they have to click for.
- If we keep working on income, test **visible inline guidance or examples** (no click required) or the monthly/annual toggle (T-08), not another tooltip.

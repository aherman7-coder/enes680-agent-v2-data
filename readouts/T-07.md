# Single-Page Personal Info
**T-07** | Northwind Travel | Personal Info | Sep 29 to Oct 27, 2025 | Internal idea

## Setup
Collapsed the three personal info screens (name, contact, address) into one long page.
Sample: 26,300 applicants, 50/50.

## Results
| Metric | Control | Treatment | Lift | Significant? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personal info step completion | 84.1% | 83.6% | -0.6% relative | No |
| Submit rate | 53.0% | 52.8% | -0.4% relative | No |

## Learnings
- No measurable effect. Mobile users scrolled past required fields and hit more validation errors (+12%).
- Prefill (T-01) remains the stronger lever on this step.
- Decision: not rolled out.

# Test Readout: Estimated Credit Line Range Before Submit
**T-06** | Branded card | Offer | Aug 18 to Sep 22, 2025 | Idea source: Internal

## What we tested
On the final screen before submit, applicants saw an estimated credit line range (e.g., "$1,000 to $5,000") based on income and soft-pull data.

## Design
- 50/50 split, branded traffic
- Sample: 58,900 applicants
- Primary metric: submit rate. Secondary: accounts booked (approved and accepted)

## Results
| Metric | Control | Treatment | Lift | Significant? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Submit rate | 55.4% | 57.7% | +4.2% relative | Yes (p < 0.01) |
| Accounts booked (per applicant) | 31.0% | 30.4% | -1.8% relative | Yes (p = 0.04) |

## Learnings
- Submits rose, but bookings fell. Approved applicants who received a line at the low end of the range declined the offer at a higher rate (19% vs 14%).
- Net effect on revenue is likely negative.
- Decision: **not rolled out.** Any version of this idea should test narrower ranges or framing that sets expectations, and must measure bookings, not just submits.

# Readout: Review Summary Card, Partner Replication
**T-09** | Harbor Market | Review & Submit | Jan 12 to Feb 9, 2026 | Idea source: Branded (replication of T-03)

## What we tested
The branded summary card from T-03, ported to the Harbor Market partner flow.

## Design
- 50/50 split, Harbor Market flow
- Sample: 18,700 applicants
- Primary metric: submit rate

## Results
| Metric | Control | Treatment | Lift | Significant? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Review step completion | 91.2% | 90.8% | -0.4% relative | No |
| Submit rate | 49.6% | 49.2% | -0.8% relative | No |

## Why it didn't replicate (team hypotheses)
- Harbor Market's review page was already shorter than the branded one (about 1.3 screens vs 2.5), so there was less length to cut.
- Partner applicants skew desktop (61%), where T-03's lift was smallest.
- Decision: not rolled out. Don't assume branded wins transfer to partner flows without checking baseline differences.

# Test Readout: Prefill Personal Info from Partner Login
**Test ID:** T-01 | **Partner:** Northwind Travel | **Step:** Personal info
**Run dates:** Mar 10 to Apr 7, 2025 | **Owner:** Product, Partner Acquisition

## Slide 1: What we tested
Applicants arriving from a logged-in Northwind Travel session saw their name, email, and mailing address prefilled. Control applicants typed everything manually.

## Slide 2: Hypothesis
Prefilling known fields will reduce effort on the personal info step, lifting step completion and overall submit rate.

## Slide 3: Design
- 50/50 split, logged-in Northwind traffic only
- Sample size: 48,200 applicants (24,100 per arm)
- Primary metric: application submit rate
- Secondary metric: personal info step completion

## Slide 4: Results
| Metric | Control | Treatment | Lift | Significant? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personal info step completion | 81.2% | 84.0% | +3.4% relative | Yes (p < 0.01) |
| Submit rate | 52.5% | 53.1% | +1.2% relative | Yes (p = 0.03) |

## Slide 5: Learnings and next steps
- Effect concentrated on mobile (+2.0% submit on mobile, flat on desktop).
- Address prefill was edited by 9% of applicants, mostly apartment numbers.
- Recommendation: roll out to 100% of logged-in Northwind traffic. Consider for other partners that pass login data.

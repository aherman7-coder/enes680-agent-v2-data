# Move Housing Payment After Income
**Test T-04 | Northwind Travel | Income & housing step | June to July 2025**
Idea came from a competitor teardown (competitor asks income first, housing second).

## Setup
Control: housing payment question appears before income.
Treatment: income first, then housing payment, same page.
Sample: 31,500 applicants, 50/50 split.

## Results
- Income & housing step completion: 83.7% control vs 85.0% treatment, **+1.5% relative lift**, significant (p = 0.02)
- Submit rate: +0.6% relative, not significant
- Time on step unchanged (median 41s both arms)

## Takeaways
- Ordering matters a little; applicants appear to stall when asked about housing cost before they've "anchored" on income.
- Rolled out to Northwind. Not yet tested on other partners.
